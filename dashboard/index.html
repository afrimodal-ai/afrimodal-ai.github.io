African speech data, by language

Early preview. Hours and permissions for 284 languages, from 75 catalogued datasets. The afrospeech-recipes Python package can load 16 of those datasets, which reach 203 of the languages. Not legal advice: check each publisher's current terms before you use its data.

      Page updated 26 November 2026. Each repository last checked for how to load it on 1 November 2026.

      • An early research preview. Figures and permissions can be wrong or out of date. Check before you rely on them.
      • Not legal advice. What a dataset allows is read from its publisher's terms, and terms change. The publisher's current terms are what bind you, not this page.
      • No audio is hosted here. Each dataset comes from its publisher. Their terms apply, and a dataset behind an access request needs your own approval.
      • Non-commercial data stays non-commercial. A model trained on a dataset that forbids commercial use inherits that restriction.
      • Hours are measured where they can be, from a publisher's metadata or each clip's audio header, and a clip listed twice counts once. Otherwise they are the publisher's own figure, which can differ from what it released. Hours whose split nobody has measured are shown apart and left out of the total.
      • Speaker counts are never added up, because one person can be in more than one split. Counts by split come from a publisher's speaker column; where a publisher only states a figure, the page quotes it.
      • Not affiliated with or endorsed by any dataset publisher named here.
