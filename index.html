About

AfriModal AI is an independent research programme on speech-capable and multimodal language technology for African languages. The central question is whether these systems work reliably for the languages, speakers and conditions they are used in, particularly where data is scarce.

Most of the work is on evaluation: building benchmarks and datasets, measuring whether meaning survives recognition, translation and generation, and finding the failures that aggregate scores hide. Applications such as healthcare communication, education and agriculture serve as testbeds for this work rather than separate lines of research.

Research

  1. Speech & Multimodal AI

    Speech recognition, spoken language understanding and speech-capable language models, including models that combine speech, text and vision.

  2. Evaluation & Reliability

    Benchmarks and test protocols that check whether outputs preserve meaning, and whether systems fail predictably across languages, dialects, accents and recording conditions.

  3. Language Infrastructure

    Speech and text datasets, tokenization, orthography and annotation standards that later work depends on.

  4. Low-Resource AI

    Methods for languages with little or almost no labelled data, and honest reporting of what is achievable at that scale.

  5. Cross-Lingual Transfer

    When knowledge from related or better-resourced languages helps, and when it introduces errors.

  6. Real-World Applications

    Testing systems in settings such as healthcare communication, where errors have consequences and evaluation has to reflect actual use.
